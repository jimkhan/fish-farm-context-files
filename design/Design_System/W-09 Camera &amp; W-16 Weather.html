Camera

Watching the site remotely is why some owners buy FMS at all. This feature depends on a camera on a pole at a rural site — so its failure states are first-class work: it degrades and says so honestly, and never implies the farm is unmonitored.

The client only ever receives a playable stream. The browser is handed a short-lived HLS (or WebRTC) playback URL. Raw RTSP addresses and camera credentials stay server-side and never reach this device — surfaced factually in the details panel below. This version is live view only: there is no recording or playback.

Camera · documented variants

Camera offline

Device unreachable

A camera on a pole at a rural site will go offline — power cuts and network drops are routine here. The placeholder is calm, tells the owner when it was last seen and the likely cause, offers Retry, and states plainly that monitoring continues through readings and alerts. A dead camera must never imply the farm is unmonitored.

Seasonal guidance — বর্ষা (monsoon)General guidance, not a live measurement
  • Oxygen risk is highest. Overcast days mean less photosynthesis; heavy rain dilutes surface oxygen. Keep aeration ready and check dawn DO closely.
  • Runoff and dilution. Rain washes in silt, fertiliser and runoff from nearby land — watch turbidity, pH swings and sudden ammonia changes after downpours.
  • Disease pressure rises. Fluctuating temperature and water quality stress fish; monitor for mortality spikes and skin/gill problems.
