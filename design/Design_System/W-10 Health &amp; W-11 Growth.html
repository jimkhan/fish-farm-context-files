ADG & SGR need two samplings
This batch has been sampled once (6.5 g on 2026-07-20). A growth rate is the change between two points in time — with a single point there is no interval to measure, so showing “0” or a trend line would be fabricated.
FCR needs a positive biomass gain
Feed has been logged (38 kg) but there is no second biomass measurement yet, so gain is unknown. FCR = feed ÷ gain is left blank rather than dividing by an assumed number.
