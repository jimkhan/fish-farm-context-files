Two more worker screens on a low-end Android, rendered as a native app. M-06 pairs mortality (near-daily) and sampling (every ~10 days) under one segmented control; M-07 is the daily task list the owner defines and the worker completes — usually with no signal, a photo as proof it happened. Keyboard-free throughout: steppers, chips and taps only, one-handed in bright sun with wet hands. 100% Bangla, Bengali numerals ০–৯, single Bangla date + ritu, fish-off mortality mark, only mg/L & pH kept Latin.
