সাইন-ইন · খাবার · পানির রিডিং

Three worker screens on a low-end Android, rendered as a native app. Karim signs in once (the only place a keyboard appears), then logs feed pond-by-pond and takes the morning water reading — everything else is steppers, chips and taps, usable one-handed in bright sun with wet hands and no signal. 100% Bangla, Bengali numerals ০–৯, a single Bangla date + ritu, fish-off mortality mark, and only mg/L & pH kept in Latin.

সাইন-ইন — Login

The absolute minimum. FMS mark, one line of what this is, email/phone + password with a show-password toggle, one big sign-in button. No public sign-up — the owner creates the account and assigns the worker to one project. After sign-in the worker lands straight in that single assigned project; there is never a project picker.

পানির রিডিং — Water Reading

The morning reading — the single most consequential thing the worker records, because it drives the oxygen alerts. The two daily essentials, দ্রবীভূত অক্সিজেন (mg/L) and তাপমাত্রা (°সে), sit on big steppers with no scrolling; the rest (pH · অ্যামোনিয়া · নাইট্রাইট · ক্ষারত্ব · সেকি) hide behind a disclosure. Below ৪ mg/L the DO field turns red and an inline danger card offers জরুরি জানান — never an equipment-control button. Chain-drag and aeration are logged right alongside.
