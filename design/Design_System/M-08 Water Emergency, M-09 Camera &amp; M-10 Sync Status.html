জরুরি · ক্যামেরা · সিংক অবস্থা

Three worker screens rendered as a native app. M-08 is the highest-stakes screen — one honest tap to raise an oxygen/water emergency to owner রহিমা খাতুন, and it must never fail offline. M-09 is a simple live camera check. M-10 makes the offline-first promise visible so nothing logged is ever in doubt. Product rule held throughout: the app can only notify — it never operates equipment. 100% Bangla, Bengali numerals ০–৯, single Bangla date + ritu, fish-off mortality mark, only mg/L & pH kept Latin.

Screen 1 · M-08

জরুরি — পানি/অক্সিজেন — Water Emergency

Fish are dying now; the worker may be panicking, in the dark, one-handed. One dominant red জরুরি button is the only thing to do. Optional zero-typing context chips help the owner but are never required. One deliberate confirmation prevents misfires without slowing a real crisis — and if there is no connection the emergency queues and still counts: “অফলাইন — সংযোগ পেলেই সাথে সাথে পাঠানো হবে”. The app only notifies; meanwhile advice (aerate manually, exchange water) is text, never a control.

The whole screen is the button. Pond এ-২ (the active problem) is pre-selected; context chips sit below, all optional.

সিংক অবস্থা — Sync / Queue Status

Where the worker can trust that nothing they logged was lost — the reassurance screen for the whole offline model. A connection + sync banner with the last successful sync, counts by state, and the queue itself (newest first) with per-item badges. A failed item retries safely with a plain reason; a conflict is honest — the server’s value was kept because the owner corrected the same record, explained rather than silently discarded. Sync is never something the worker manages by hand.

ALL SYNCEDnothing pending

Clean and reassuring — everything is up on the server, nothing waiting. The calm baseline the worker should see most days.
