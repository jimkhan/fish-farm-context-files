Reports · documented variants

Zero-activity day

A quiet day reports zeros — not an error

On a day with no logs the summary shows honest zeros and a water status of OK. It must never look broken, and must never invent a worse status just because data is missing. Missing logs mean "nothing happened", not "something is wrong".
